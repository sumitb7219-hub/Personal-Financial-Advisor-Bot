FinPulse AI — System Architecture & Decision Pipeline

This view demonstrates how the prototype processes user inputs through deterministic financial formulas and heuristic decision trees to generate personalized financial planning advice.

STAGE 01

Slot & Intent Parser

Extracts numeric values (₹, K, Lakh) and intent keywords from natural chat input or slider events and normalizes them into the state store.

STAGE 02

Quantitative Ratio Engine

Computes Net Surplus, Savings Rate (%), Debt-to-Income Ratio (DTI %), and Emergency Runway coverage against 6-month outflow benchmarks.

STAGE 03

Risk & Age Matrix

Applies age-adjusted equity ceilings (`100 - Age` baseline modified by risk appetite and emergency fund deficit overrides).

STAGE 04

Advisory Synthesizer

Evaluates priority rules (Cashflow Deficit > High DTI > Low Emergency Fund > Goal SIPs) to output concrete monthly action steps.

Core Mathematical & Heuristic Rules

                    

                        

                            
1.Composite FinHealth Score (0–100)

                            Score = SavingsPts(35) + DTIPts(35) + EmergencyPts(30)
                            
Penalizes DTI > 35% heavily and rewards Savings Rate ≥ 25% and Emergency Reserve ≥ 6 months.

                        


                        

                            
2. Dynamic Emergency Fund Override

                            If EmergencyRunway < 3 Months → Divert 35–50% Surplus to Liquid Fund
                            
Prevents premature illiquid market exposure until baseline liquidity safety is achieved.

                        


                        

                            
3. Future Value of Monthly SIP

                            FV = P × [((1 + r)^n - 1) / r] × (1 + r)
                            
Where r = AnnualCAGR / 12 and n = Months, plus optional annual 10% step-up compounding.
